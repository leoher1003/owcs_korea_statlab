==Information==
===Format===
*'''Regular Season:''' ''March 20<sup>th</sup> - April 19<sup>th</sup>''
**Round Robin 
**All matches are {{Ft|3}}
**{{Bgcolortext|up|Top 4 teams}} advance to '''Playoffs Seeding Decider Matches'''
**{{Bgcolortext|stayup|Next 4 teams}} advance to '''Last Chance Qualifier'''
*'''Playoffs Seeding Decider Matches:''' ''April 24<sup>th</sup> - 26<sup>th</sup>''
**Round robin
**All matches are {{Ft|3}}
**{{Bgcolortext|up|All teams}} advance to '''Regional Playoffs'''
**{{Bgcolortext|byeup|Top 2 teams}} receive a first-round bye
*'''Last Chance Qualifier:''' ''April 24<sup>th</sup> - 26<sup>th</sup>''
**Double elimination bracket
**All matches are {{Ft|3}}
**{{Bgcolortext|stayup|5th and 6th team from Regular Season}} start from Upper Bracket
**{{Bgcolortext|up|Top 2 teams}} advance to '''Regional Playoffs'''
*'''Regional Playoffs:''' ''May 1<sup>st</sup> - 3<sup>rd</sup>''
**Single elimination bracket
**All matches are {{Ft|4}}
**{{Bgcolortext|up|Top 4 teams}} advance to {{LeagueIconSmall/owcs/asia}} [[Overwatch Champions Series/2026/Asia/Stage 1|OWCS Asia Stage 1 Championship]]
*'''Hero Bans'''
**Each team bans 1 hero per map, with the ban impacting both teams
**Each ban must be from a different role than the other team's ban
**Teams can only ban a hero once per series, but may repeat a ban from the opposing team
**The higher seeded team or team that has lost the last map chooses to ban first or second
